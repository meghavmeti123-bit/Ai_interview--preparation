          PRACTICE

          Use a clear answer structure

          Explain the situation, your reasoning, the action you took, and the result. Keep examples specific and distinguish your contribution from the team's.

          TECHNICAL

          Make your trade-offs explicit

          Clarify requirements, compare options, call out risks, and describe how you would verify the result.

          REFLECTION

          Review the explanation, not just the score

          Use answer explanations to identify a next learning step. Scores describe this practice session, not a guaranteed hiring outcome.
